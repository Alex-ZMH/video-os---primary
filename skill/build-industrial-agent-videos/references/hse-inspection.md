# Chemical HSE inspection profile

Use this profile with `workflow.md` when the case covers chemical-production HSE inspection, compliance, work permits, evidence, or remediation closure.

## Story order

Keep the causal chain visible:

1. HSE scope and source material: laws, company rules, permits, equipment state, photos, and inspection records.
2. Versioned knowledge intake and fast retrieval of current requirements.
3. Conversion of hazardous-chemical, hot-work, confined-space, and work-at-height requirements into executable checks.
4. Suspicion detection from photos, records, equipment properties, and realtime state.
5. A traceable evidence chain linking permits, photos, equipment data, and inspection records to each conclusion.
6. Risk grade, governing clause,现场 evidence, and review advice.
7. Remediation owner and deadline.
8. Recheck and closure tracking.
9. User-supplied project outcomes plus the human responsibility boundary.

Lead with the closed-loop value, not document inventory. Do not invent regulations, customer names, measured gains, or accuracy figures. Write numbers for natural Chinese pronunciation in TTS while keeping exact figures on screen.

## Safety boundary

Always state that high-risk conclusions require human review and that the agent does not replace statutory approval or on-site safety responsibility. Do not depict unsafe behavior, missing PPE, uncontrolled hot work, or workers inside a hazardous area merely for visual drama.

## Visual strategy

Use credible chemical-plant control rooms, field inspections, equipment, permits, rugged phones or tablets, and remediation reviews. Three strong native plates per aspect ratio may cover adjacent workflow beats when the changing evidence or action is carried by editable overlays; do not multiply near-duplicate generated images without a narrative need. Portrait plates must be generated or outpainted independently.

## Voice and timing

Nine short paragraphs grouped into three Xiachen WAV files usually balances continuity and replaceability. Generate and transcribe the first group as a candidate before the full pass. Use measured WAV durations, retain about 0.5 seconds between groups, and keep the 1.2-second brand tail from the parent workflow.
