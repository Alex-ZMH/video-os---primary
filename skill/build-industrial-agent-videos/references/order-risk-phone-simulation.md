# Order-fulfillment risk phone simulation

Use this reference for industrial order-delivery risk cases shown as paired 16:9 and 9:16 phone simulations.

## Story spine

Keep one customer order as the thread: customer question → cross-department blind spots → order-linked data → risk detection → cause/consequence/time window → owner/action → human approval → acceptance targets.

## Phone UI

- Show only decision-useful fields: order, due date, owner, risk level, BOM/material shortage, purchase ETA, schedule/equipment, inspection/logistics, consequence, time window, and optional actions.
- Mark invented operational records and identifiers as `仿真演示数据`.
- Keep schedule changes, expedite decisions, substitute materials, and customer commitments behind explicit human confirmation.

## Evidence language

- Treat source-provided future metrics as `建设与验收目标` or `目标`, never as realized results.
- Do not invent achieved savings, accuracy, OTD uplift, or delay reduction.

## Paired delivery

- Generate portrait-native backgrounds rather than cropping landscape images.
- Generate the final Xiachen narration once, copy it to the other aspect-ratio project, and verify matching SHA-256 hashes.
- Share narration and scene timing across both projects; validate both before requesting approval.
- After approval, render sequentially with one worker and low-memory mode, then verify codec, resolution, duration, audio, and the pair check.
